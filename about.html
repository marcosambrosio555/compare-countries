About CompareCountries

A free, open comparison tool for exploring how the world's countries stack up against each other.

What is CompareCountries?

CompareCountries is a lightweight web app that lets anyone — students, teachers, researchers or the simply curious — compare countries side by side across six metrics: area, population, population density, GDP, GDP per capita and life expectancy.

Every comparison page shares the same tools: a searchable dashboard with four visual modes (draggable blocks, bubbles, columns and cards), a ranked bar chart, head-to-head statistics, fun real-world context facts, combined totals, and a live world ranking.

Data & methodology

Figures are aggregated and simplified from public datasets (World Bank, United Nations, IMF and national statistics offices) for educational comparison. Nominal GDP is expressed in US dollars and is not adjusted for purchasing power parity (PPP). Life expectancy refers to average life expectancy at birth. Population density is calculated automatically from the area and population figures already in the dataset.

This project favours clarity and approximate, easy-to-compare figures over frequent live updates — it is best treated as a teaching and exploration tool rather than an authoritative statistical source.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

Country figures for area, population, GDP, GDP per capita and life expectancy are aggregated and approximated from public sources such as the World Bank, United Nations and IMF, then simplified for educational comparison. They are refreshed periodically but are not a live feed, so treat them as close estimates rather than an official statistical release.

Why do some countries show "—" for GDP or life expectancy?

A handful of very small or data-scarce territories don't have a reliable public figure for every metric. Rather than guess, the tool shows a dash so you're never misled by an invented number.

How is population density calculated?

Density is calculated automatically as population ÷ area for every country, so it always stays consistent with the area and population figures shown elsewhere in the tool.

Can I share a comparison with someone else?

Yes — press "Share comparison" on any comparison page. It copies a link containing your exact list of countries, so opening that link rebuilds the same comparison automatically.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free, with no account required. Clearly marked, reserved advertising spaces are included in the layout for future support of the project, but no tracking-heavy ad scripts are loaded by default.

I found an error in the data — can I report it?

This is an independent student project built for learning and portfolio purposes. There's no formal reporting channel yet, but the dataset is being improved over time.
