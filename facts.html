Fun Geography & Economics Facts

Bite-sized, surprising comparisons between countries — perfect for trivia night, classrooms, or idle curiosity.

Reserved advertising space (top)
🗺️

Russia could swallow Pluto

At 17.1 million km², Russia's land area is actually larger than the surface area of the dwarf planet Pluto (about 16.6 million km²).

🇻🇦

Two Vatican Cities fit in Monaco

Vatican City (0.44 km²) is so small that roughly 4-5 of them would fit inside Monaco's 2.02 km² footprint.

👶

India adds a small country every year

India's population grows by roughly the population of a mid-sized European country every single year.

🏙️

Singapore is denser than most cities

As a whole country, Singapore is about as densely populated as many of the world's biggest individual cities.

💵

One US state economy rivals many countries

California's economy alone is larger than the entire nominal GDP of most countries in Africa combined.

🧓

The longevity gap is huge

The difference between the highest and lowest national life expectancy today is still more than three decades.

🏝️

Micro-states, mega influence

Countries like Luxembourg and Monaco have tiny populations and land area but some of the highest GDP per capita figures on Earth.

🌍

Africa's size is often underestimated

Africa is so large that the United States, China, India, Japan and most of Europe could all fit inside its outline at once.

🐫

Empty is relative

Mongolia has more livestock than people — its population density is among the lowest of any sovereign country.

⚽

Football-field thinking

Whole countries can be measured in football pitches: Vatican City is only about 60 pitches large; Russia is billions.
