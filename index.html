    About comparing countries by area

    Area tells you how much land — and inland water — a country actually occupies, measured in square kilometres. It's the oldest and most intuitive way to compare countries: Russia alone is bigger than the whole of Pluto's surface, while Vatican City could fit inside a large shopping mall car park.

    Use the search box above to add countries to your comparison. Every tab below reads the same list, so you can flip between a visual dashboard, ranked bar charts, head-to-head statistics, fun real-world comparisons, combined totals and the live world ranking without losing your selection.

    How to use this tool

    • Search and add up to 24 countries using the form above.
    • Switch between Blocks, Bubbles, Columns and Cards in the Dashboard tab — drag blocks around freely.
    • Click a chip in the list to focus it in the Statistics tab.
    • Click the small colour dot to reassign a country's colour.
    • Use Share comparison to copy a link that reopens your exact selection.

    Quick facts

    🇷🇺

    Biggest on Earth

    Russia spans 17,098,242 km² — more than 11% of the planet's entire land area.

    🇻🇦

    Smallest state

    Vatican City covers just 0.44 km², making Russia about 39 million times larger.

    🌍

    Africa's giant

    Algeria is Africa's largest country by area, at over 2.38 million km².
