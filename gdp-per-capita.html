    About comparing countries by gdp per capita

    GDP per capita divides a country's total economic output by its population. It's a widely used (if imperfect) proxy for average living standards — small, wealthy states like Luxembourg and Monaco often top the charts, ahead of much larger economies.

    Because it's an average, GDP per capita can hide big gaps between rich and poor within the same country — but it remains one of the fastest ways to compare relative prosperity between nations.

    Quick facts

    🇱🇺

    Small but rich

    Luxembourg has one of the highest GDP per capita figures in the world, above US$130,000.

    ⚖️

    Averages hide inequality

    GDP per capita is a mean, not a median — it can be skewed upward by a small, very wealthy group.
