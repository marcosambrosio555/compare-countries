    About comparing countries by gdp

    Gross Domestic Product (GDP) is the total monetary value of all goods and services a country produces in a year. It's the number economists reach for first when talking about the 'size' of an economy — the United States, China, Germany and Japan currently sit at the top of the table.

    Nominal GDP doesn't adjust for the local cost of living, so pair it with the GDP per Capita comparison for a fuller picture of prosperity, not just scale.

    How to use this tool

    • Search and add up to 24 countries using the form above.
    • Switch between Blocks, Bubbles, Columns and Cards in the Dashboard tab — drag blocks around freely.
    • Click a chip in the list to focus it in the Statistics tab.
    • Click the small colour dot to reassign a country's colour.
    • Use Share comparison to copy a link that reopens your exact selection.
