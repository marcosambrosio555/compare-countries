Why rankings change over time

Country statistics are never perfectly static. Population estimates are revised as new census and UN data are released, GDP figures shift with exchange rates and growth, and even land area can change slightly as territorial disputes are resolved or coastlines are resurveyed. The figures here are a snapshot for comparison and educational purposes, not an official statistical release.

Want to dig into a specific pair of countries instead of the world top 10? Head to any of the comparison pages linked in the menu above and build a custom side-by-side comparison — your selection is saved to the page URL so you can bookmark or share it.
